# Photo brief

The biggest upgrade left for the site is photography. Right now the site has two phone photos
that came through email at about 600 pixels, which is too small to look sharp on modern
phones. Here's how to get photos that match the look of the site.

## What we need

| Shot | Used for | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A pile or plate of cookies, close up | Home page hero (the arch) | Fill the frame. Portrait (taller than wide). |
| This week's Move-In flavor | The dark Move-In section | Shot from straight above works best (it's shown in a circle). |
| One photo of each item: Chocolate Chip, Caramel Cone Crunch, S'mores, Fudge Brownie, Brownie Bites, Banana Bread Bites | Menu cards | Square. Same setup for all of them so the menu looks like a set. |
| Optional: hands holding a box, or a pickup on campus | Future "about" section | Makes it feel like a neighbor. |

## How to shoot them

- **Light:** next to a window, daylight, no overhead lights, no flash. Turn off the kitchen lights so the colors stay warm.
- **Surface:** pick one and use it for everything: a cream linen towel, parchment or a wooden board. Avoid the granite counter and the wire rack (busy patterns compete with the cookies).
- **Angle:** straight from above for flat items, or a low 45° angle for stacks. Keep the angle the same across the menu.
- **Framing:** fill the frame with food. Nothing else on the counter (toaster, glasses, spatulas).
- **A break:** one cookie broken in half shows the inside. It sells.
- **Settings:** take them with the phone's main camera (1x, not zoomed), in the highest quality.

## How to send them

Send the **original files**, not screenshots and not pasted into a document (that shrinks
them). AirDrop, Google Drive or "Send as file / Actual size" in email all work. Name them after
the item if you can (`caramel-cone-crunch.jpg`).
